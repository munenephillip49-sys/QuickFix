ABOUT QUICKFIX

Local problems.
Smarter fixes.

QuickFix is built to make finding help easier. Instead of wasting time searching through random contacts, people can connect with service providers through one modern platform.

01 — THE IDEA

Make getting help simple.

Whether someone needs a plumber, electrician, phone technician, computer specialist, mechanic or another service provider, QuickFix is designed to make the process clearer and faster.

02 — THE COMMUNITY

Give providers an opportunity.

QuickFix is not only for customers. Skilled people can create provider profiles, receive service requests and build their reputation through completed work and customer reviews.

What makes QuickFix different?

Built around real jobs

The platform focuses on connecting people with actual service needs instead of simply being another directory.

Two-sided experience

Customers and providers get experiences designed around what they actually need to accomplish.

Built for trust

Profiles, availability, communication, job statuses and reviews help create a better service experience.

Need something fixed?

Find a service provider and start your request through QuickFix. Or, if you have a skill people need, join the provider network.
