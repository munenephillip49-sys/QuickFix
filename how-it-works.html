How QuickFix works

From problem
to solution.

QuickFix makes it easier to find the right person, explain what you need and keep track of the job from request to completion.

STEP 01
🔎

Find

Search for the service you need and explore available providers.

STEP 02
📩

Request

Tell the provider what needs to be done, share your location and send your request.

STEP 03
✅

Fix

Once accepted, communicate with the provider and get the job completed.

For customers

Need something fixed?

You don't need to search through random contacts or social media posts. QuickFix puts the service discovery and request process in one place.

  • ✓ Search for a service
  • ✓ Compare available providers
  • ✓ Send a service request
  • ✓ Share your location
  • ✓ Communicate with the provider
  • ✓ Track your request
For providers

Have a skill?

Turn your skill into opportunities by becoming a QuickFix provider and receiving requests from people who need your service.

  • ✓ Create your provider profile
  • ✓ Choose when you're available
  • ✓ Receive customer requests
  • ✓ Accept or decline jobs
  • ✓ Communicate with customers
  • ✓ Track your earnings

What happens after you send a request?

1
Provider receives the request The provider gets a notification with the customer's job details.
2
Provider accepts or declines Providers can decide whether they can take the job.
3
Both sides can communicate Once accepted, the customer and provider can communicate about the job.
4
The job gets completed The request progresses until the work is finished.
5
Review the experience Customers can rate their experience after the job.
